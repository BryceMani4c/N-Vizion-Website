Who We Are

N-Vizion Church is a Spirit-filled community rooted in the Word of God, located in Sparta, Tennessee. Under the leadership of Pastor Derek and Jody Bolinger, we exist to make the Kingdom of God visible in every life we touch.

02
Our Values

We value the uncompromised Word, authentic community, passionate worship, and Spirit-led ministry. Every person who walks through our doors matters — because where everybody is somebody.

03
Our Mission

To preach the Gospel of the Kingdom without compromise, make disciples who make disciples, and build a house where every person encounters the living God and finds their God-given purpose.

Got Questions?

Frequently
Asked

What should I expect on my first visit?
Expect a warm welcome, Spirit-led worship, and a Word-based message that applies to real life. Services typically run about 90 minutes. Dress however you're comfortable — we care about your heart, not your outfit.
When and where do you meet?
We gather every Sunday at 11:00 AM at 180 Churchill Drive, Sparta, TN 38583. Doors open at 10:30 AM — come early, stay late, and bring someone with you.
Is there anything for my kids?
We love families. Reach out to us through the "I'm New" form and we'll let you know exactly what we have available for your children during services.
Can I watch services online?
We are not currently streaming or posting services online, but that is coming soon. Follow us on Facebook for updates and announcements as we get that set up.
How can I give or support the ministry?
You can give online through our Give page. Every gift goes directly toward advancing the Kingdom of God through N-Vizion Church's ministry and community outreach.
How do I get connected with the team?
Visit our "I'm New" page, fill out the short form, and a member of our team will personally reach out to you. We want to know your name before you walk through the door.
