Let’s Review

Being
Planted

“Those who are planted in the house of the Lord shall flourish in the courts of our God.”

Psalm 92:13

Your potential and purpose start off in seed-form. Start with what you have — take just the next step. Build roots and cultivate your potential by being a part of our N-Vizion Church family.

Being planted means to take root. A seed turns into harvest when it remains in the environment that nurtures its growth. We believe as you grow roots here at N-Vizion, that you will begin to flourish in every area of your life.

We want to see you thriving in your personal life, in your calling, and in your relationships.

A healthy church is a healthy body.

All of us were created by God to make a difference in the life of another person. God has a place for you where your unique abilities and passions can touch the lives of others. We believe that your life will never make sense until you find, develop, and fulfill that purpose.

Our Primary Purpose

Be a Witness

One of our primary purposes is to reach people far from God. Every one of us can do our part by following these four principles to win people to Christ.

01

Personal Responsibility

Accept that it is your role — not someone else’s — to reach the people God has placed in your life. The harvest is ready; the laborers are needed.

02

Personal Relationship

Build a genuine connection with people before you preach at them. Love people where they are. Relationship is the bridge the Gospel walks across.

03

Personal Story

Share your personal testimony. Nobody can argue with what God has done in your own life. Your story is your most powerful tool — use it freely.

04

Personal Invitation

Give a personal invitation to join you here at N-Vizion. Warmly welcome people in. This is where Everybody Is Somebody — let’s fill these seats together.
