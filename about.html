Our Story

Everybody Is Somebody  •  We Love You Too Much to Leave You As You Are

Who We Are

A People Called
to the Kingdom

N-Vizion Church is a non-denominational body of believers in Sparta, Tennessee. Everybody is somebody here — regardless of background, tradition, or where you've been. We welcome you as you are, and we love you too much to leave you that way. The Kingdom of God changes people, and we are committed to preaching it without compromise.

01

The Gospel

We preach the death, burial, and resurrection of Jesus Christ. The Gospel is the power of God for transformation — and we deliver it without compromise, without apology, in every season.

02

The Kingdom

We believe God's Kingdom is not only coming — it is here now. We live and preach the rule and reign of Jesus Christ on earth as it is in heaven.

03

Family

We are not a program — we are a family. Everybody is somebody here. We carry each other's burdens, celebrate each other's breakthroughs, and nobody walks alone.

Mission

Why We Exist

To preach the Gospel of the Kingdom — the rule and reign of Jesus Christ — and to make disciples who are transformed by it. We believe everybody is somebody, and we love people too much to leave them where they are. Every message, every service, every connection exists to see lives changed by the power of God.

We believe Matthew 6:33 is not just a verse to memorize — it is a way of life. We seek first His Kingdom, trusting that He provides everything else. Every service, every message, every connection is ordered around that one pursuit.

"But seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added to you."

Matthew 6:33

Our Values

What Drives Us

01

The Word, Uncompromised

We hold the Bible as the inspired, infallible Word of God — the final authority for how we live, think, and lead. We preach it without apology, in every season.

02

Spirit-Led, Kingdom-Focused

We believe in a Spirit-filled life that reflects heaven on earth. We pursue the presence of God in every gathering, not just a program or performance.

03

Radical Hospitality

04

Eternity in View

Everything we do is shaped by eternity. Our greatest goal is to make heaven our home — and to bring as many people with us as we possibly can.
